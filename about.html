About this project

What this is

ASTO is a word puzzle: sixteen tiles, four hidden analogy sets — this is to that as that is to this. It is also a capstone project for a class on multi-agent AI development, built by Max Rowe.

How the puzzles are made

The puzzles are generated by an AI authoring pipeline that Max built, prompted, and edits — a team of specialized agents that write, check, and critique each board before he ever sees it.

Nothing ships sight unseen: every published puzzle has been played by Max before release, and approved — or approved with changes — by him. The AI drafts; he decides.
