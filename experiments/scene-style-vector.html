Style spike — flat vector, not pixels

The pixel constraint traces to one phrase in the original ask ("low spec, pixelated or something", 2026-08-19) and was never re-examined. Duolingo — the stated inspiration — is emphatically not pixel art: it's flat vector, bold simple shapes, expression through pose rather than resolution. This sheet asks one question: is flat vector closer? Same band footprint (375×60), same cat, same three states — three rendering styles.

4 · Palettes

Why this is architecturally fine. The old ticket claimed palette-index pixel grids were "the only shape that fits" the Studio's seams. That was overstated — the real constraint is text, not binaries, and SVG satisfies it just as well: it is data, it goes through a store like anything else, it needs no dependency, and it scales to any size crisply (see the logo row — one source, four sizes). It also plausibly raises the agent's ceiling: an LLM asked for a 3,600-cell index grid produces noise, while SVG shapes are a format it can actually reason about. Motion becomes CSS/SMIL on named parts (an ear, a tail) rather than repainting frames.

What this spike is not. Not production art, not a character design signed off, not 18 scenes. One register, three styles, one question: is this direction closer than pixels? If none of the three lands, that is a useful answer too — and cheap.
