DISPOSABLE EXPERIMENT

Sound audition

Four palettes for the three moments: select a tile, solve a set, make a mistake. All synthesized live, no audio files. Listen on the cards, then use the board at the bottom to feel what fifteen taps in a row actually sound like.

Your mix

Paper select, ceramic solve, woodblock mistake. Three materials off one cafe table, which is why it hangs together, but each palette was balanced against itself and never against the others. Trim them until the select disappears under the solve, then play the two sequences: those are the only combinations the game will ever actually produce.

Solve candidates

Six ideas for what a set coming home should sound like, not six shades of one. Tap a name to hear it; Use puts it into the mix above so it can be heard after four real taps. Some rise and some fall, which is the biggest difference here: rising reads as well done, falling as that is where it goes.

The palettes

The four candidates on their own, each balanced internally.

Tap test

Pick a palette, then tap freely. This is the question the cards cannot answer: a sound that charms once has to survive a whole board.

What to listen for. Select fires most often by far, so it has to be nearly invisible. Solve is the reward and can afford to be the prettiest thing here. Mistake is the one with a trap: none of these is a buzzer, on purpose, because the GDD's mistake pips are coffee beans and never red. If a mistake sound reads as punishment rather than a gentle "not that," say so and it gets softened.
