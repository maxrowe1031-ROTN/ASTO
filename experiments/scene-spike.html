What this spike deliberately skips. No agent, no store, no validator, no register map, no tests — the grid below is inline data and the painter is ~40 lines. If a band this short does not read as a place, the rest of the register-scene plan is discarded having cost this one file.

What this pass actually found. First Light, the harbor, and the mountain all read clearly as their register once two real bugs were fixed (an invisible sail, a mountain peak tall enough to clip its own summit off-canvas). The bakery does not — two different structural attempts at a shopfront both read as abstract blocks rather than a building, at both grid sizes. That is the horizon-band grammar's actual limit surfacing, not a drawing-skill problem to iterate away quietly: a landscape has a horizon to organise around, and a street-level scene does not. Worth carrying into Phase 3 rather than reworking here. The same session also found one bug three times in three shapes: the palest token in the palette is invisible against the sky band it shares that exact color with, so a snow cap, then a chimney's smoke, both vanished until given a token that contrasts with its LOCAL background, not merely one that is in the six-token set.

What round 2 found. The cat renders and reads in all 24 combinations (4 scenes × 3 sizes × 2 colours), verified by decoding canvas pixels — including the bakery, where a character in front of the counter visibly anchors what the horizon grammar couldn't. White survives every scene; black sinks into the mountain's slopes and the harbor's dark water, exactly as the dark-ground-underfoot argument predicted. The three states read at sprite scale: a hop with one gold sparkle, a flattened ears-down miss, an auto-return to idle. Two honest corrections from pixel-decoding the palette claims: the harbor's ember is the WATER band (sunset-lit, rows 11–13), not the sail; and the mountain's cream is the sky glow band — round 1's abandoned snow cap is still not drawn, the new palette merely makes one possible. Round 1's rAF lesson held and was instrumented this time: window.__spikeTest renders single frames synchronously so a hidden pane can still be verified.
