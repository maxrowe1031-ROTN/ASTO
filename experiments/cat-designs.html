DISPOSABLE SPIKE

Cat designs — six characters, one palette

The last sheet varied rendering (colour, outline) but held the design constant — one cat shown three ways, which is why it still read as generic. This sheet does the opposite: six genuinely different characters, all in the same soft palette and all front-facing, so the only variable is the design itself. Proportion, pose, face, and personality all change. Pick a direction — or tell me none of them is it, which is also cheap information.

1 · The six, large

Same colours throughout, deliberately — judge shape and character, not palette.

1 · The LoafBread-cat, no legs. Peak cozy; the most unmistakably cat pose there is.
2 · Chunky SitRound, soft, approachable. The safe mascot middle-ground.
3 · Big HeadHead-forward chibi. Most Duolingo-like; expression has the most room.
4 · GeometricCircles and triangles only. Reads as a logo already; least warm.
5 · Tall & ElegantUpright, poised, a little aloof. Grown-up; less huggable.
6 · Sleepy CurlCurled and asleep. Calmest of all — but hard to animate three states from.

What to react to. Proportion (how big is the head vs the body?), posture (compact and cozy, or upright and poised?), face (dots, or something with more expression?), and how much of it survives at band size in section 2. A design that reads at 46px tall is the one that can carry three states; a design that only works large is a poster, not a mascot.

Deliberately not varied here: colour, outline weight, scene style — all held at the "soft & cozy" setting so the comparison is honest. Once a design wins, the art direction around it (palette, world, level of detail) is the next question, not this one.
