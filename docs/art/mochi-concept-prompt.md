# ASTO — Mochi Concept Prompt

Create a **character concept sheet** for a recurring mascot character for a word puzzle game called ASTO.

The character's name is **Mochi**.

Mochi is a **cute white cat mascot** designed for a friendly, modern puzzle-game interface. Mochi should feel **cozy, clever, encouraging, curious, and slightly mischievous**.

Mochi is not simply decorative. Mochi is an active character who can **react to the player, interact with the environment, and creatively participate in each puzzle-board scene**.

## Personality

Mochi is:

- curious
- playful
- clever
- affectionate
- occasionally mischievous
- highly expressive
- enthusiastic when the player succeeds
- visibly disappointed, confused, or concerned when the player misses
- comfortable getting distracted by interesting things in the environment
- obsessed with **coffee**

Mochi's emotions should be extremely readable through the face, ears, tail, posture, and whole body.

Mochi should have the **emotional expressiveness of a great animated creature mascot, similar in spirit to how Pikachu communicates huge emotions through simple expressions and body language**, while remaining completely original in visual design.

Mochi does not speak. Personality is communicated visually.

## Mochi and the World

Mochi can exist naturally in **any ASTO environment**.

Rather than simply standing in front of a background, Mochi should creatively interact with each setting.

For example, Mochi might:

- sit beside a campfire
- peer over a rock
- nap beneath a tree
- climb onto an object
- chase something moving through the scene
- investigate an unusual object
- hide partially behind scenery
- play in snow
- get caught in the rain
- ride in a small boat
- stare into space from another planet
- curl up beside a fireplace
- interact with creatures or objects unique to the environment

Mochi should feel like **the same character going on a new little adventure with the player every day**.

The environment can influence Mochi's behavior, pose, props, and activity, but it should **never change Mochi's core appearance**.

## Coffee

Mochi **loves coffee**.

Coffee can become a recurring visual joke and personality trait throughout the game.

Mochi may occasionally:

- hold a tiny coffee cup
- sit beside a mug
- investigate someone's coffee
- fall asleep next to an empty cup
- appear unusually energized after drinking coffee
- protect a cup of coffee from environmental chaos
- discover coffee in increasingly improbable locations

Coffee should appear as an occasional recurring motif rather than being present in every scene.

## Core Visual Identity

- Species: **white cat**
- Name: **Mochi**
- Signature feature: **small red scarf**
- Fur: pure or slightly warm white
- Style: **clean 2D mascot illustration**
- Tone: cute, charming, soft, approachable
- Shape language: rounded, compact, simple silhouette
- Body: small, slightly chubby, compact proportions
- Head: slightly oversized relative to body
- Ears: upright triangular ears with soft pink inner ears
- Eyes: medium-large expressive oval eyes
- Face: tiny nose, small mouth, simple whiskers
- Tail: curved, highly expressive, and readable in silhouette
- Scarf: recognizable red scarf that remains consistent across appearances
- Rendering: flat or lightly shaded
- Linework: clean, soft, polished
- Readability: instantly recognizable at small size

## Expressiveness

Mochi's entire body should participate in emotion.

Use:

- ear position
- tail position
- eye shape
- mouth shape
- body posture
- paw gestures
- head tilt
- squash-and-stretch posing

Mochi should be capable of exaggerated but charming reactions such as:

- ecstatic celebration
- proud satisfaction
- curiosity
- intense concentration
- confusion
- skepticism
- surprise
- embarrassment
- disappointment
- determination
- sleepiness
- impatience
- playful mischief

Expressions should remain readable even when Mochi appears fairly small within a game scene.

## Animation Suitability

The design must work well for recurring animation states.

### Idle
Possible behaviors include:

- blinking
- tail swishing
- ear twitching
- stretching
- looking around
- sitting down
- grooming
- becoming distracted by something nearby
- sipping or investigating coffee

### Correct Answer

Mochi may:

- perk up
- smile
- hop
- raise the tail
- clap paws together
- celebrate
- look proudly at the player

### Big Success

Mochi may:

- leap into the air
- spin
- strike an exaggerated victory pose
- become extremely excited
- interact excitedly with nearby scenery

### Miss

Mochi may:

- flatten the ears slightly
- look confused
- wince
- slump
- tilt the head
- glance skeptically toward the player
- recover and encourage another attempt

The reaction should feel playful rather than punishing.

## Important Design Goals

The character should emphasize:

- strong silhouette
- clear emotional readability
- simple repeatable features
- visual consistency
- ease of posing
- ease of animation
- ability to interact naturally with environments
- recognizable personality
- friendly puzzle-game energy

Mochi should feel like a character players gradually become attached to, rather than merely an interface decoration.

## Avoid

- realistic anatomy
- detailed fur texture
- complex clothing
- excessive accessories
- stripes or tabby markings
- changing the scarf design
- changing Mochi's proportions between poses
- overly tiny facial features
- visual resemblance to existing famous mascot characters
- passive poses in every scene
- making Mochi feel like a sticker pasted on top of an environment

## Concept Exploration

Show **6 distinct design variations** of Mochi on one concept sheet.

Every design must preserve:

- white fur
- red scarf
- compact rounded proportions
- expressive face
- simple 2D mascot aesthetic

Explore moderate variation in:

- head shape
- body roundness
- eye shape
- muzzle design
- tail shape
- scarf shape
- paw design
- degree of squash-and-stretch expressiveness

Include mostly front or 3/4 seated poses, with a few expressive poses where helpful.

At least one concept should show Mochi interacting with a **small coffee cup** to demonstrate personality.

Arrange all concepts cleanly on a simple neutral background.

The finished image should resemble a **professional mascot exploration sheet for a game-art pipeline**, with enough visual clarity to choose one definitive version of Mochi for future production.
